# [Known Issue] Credentials Vault — Access Disruption Under Investigation
Date: 2026-08-12
Audience: All Staff
From: DWP Support Team

---

We are aware that some users are currently unable to access the shared credentials vault. This issue is confirmed and is being actively investigated as a priority.

**What is happening**
The credentials vault is returning an access error for affected users. This appears to be linked to permission changes applied during the recent technology rollout.

**Temporary workaround**
If you urgently need access to specific credentials, please contact your line manager or a colleague with vault access to retrieve them on your behalf while we work to restore full access.

**If you are affected, please send us the following**
- Your username and the device you are using
- The exact error message you see, or a screenshot
- The date and time you first noticed the issue

**Next update**
We will provide a further update by 15:00 today. We are not in a position to confirm a resolution time at this stage, but this is being treated as a high-priority incident.

We apologise for the disruption and thank you for your patience.

— DWP Support Team
