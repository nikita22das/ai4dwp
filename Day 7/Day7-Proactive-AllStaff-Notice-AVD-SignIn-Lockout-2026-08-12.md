Subject: Proactive Notice: AVD Sign-In and Account Lockout Issue

We are aware of an increase in AVD sign-in and account lockout issues this morning, and our support teams are actively working on it now.

While we investigate, please try these steps:
1. Sign out fully, wait 60 seconds, then sign in again slowly and make sure Caps Lock is off.
2. If you recently changed your password, use the new one and restart your laptop once before trying again.

If you still cannot sign in after 2 attempts, please contact the Service Desk and share: your username, exact error message, time of last failed sign-in, device name, and whether your password was changed recently.

Please stay calm. We know this is disruptive, and we are treating it as a priority.

Next update: within 60 minutes, even if we are still working through resolution.
