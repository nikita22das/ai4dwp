The migration is landing well for most colleagues, and the overall user experience has improved week over week. The early disruption around sign-in access, account lockouts, missing cloud files, and unstable remote connectivity has largely settled, and many teams now report a smoother day-to-day experience. In plain terms, the program has moved from widespread onboarding friction to mostly stable operations, with confidence returning across much of the business.

Two issues remain open and are being treated as priorities. First, the floor 3 printer problem has persisted longer than acceptable and is now affecting team confidence; this is being escalated with direct ownership and a near-term containment plan so users are not blocked while the permanent fix is completed. Second, a newer issue has emerged with large spreadsheet work in Finance, where app stability and slower start-of-day readiness are impacting productivity; teams are running focused validation checks, isolating the recent policy changes most closely linked in timing, and using a controlled rollback approach for affected users to restore performance while root-cause work continues. We will keep leaders updated on progress at regular intervals and communicate clearly if timelines change.
