## AVD sign-in and account lockout
Try these steps first:
1. Sign out fully, wait 60 seconds, then sign in again slowly and make sure Caps Lock is off.
2. If you recently changed your password, use the new one and restart your laptop once before trying again.

Contact the service desk if you still cannot sign in after 2 attempts, or if you see an account locked message. Share: your username, exact error text on screen, time of last failed sign-in, device name, and whether password was changed recently.

## Shared drive access denied (S: / Finance)
Try these steps first:
1. Disconnect and reconnect VPN, then open File Explorer and try the S: drive again.
2. Restart your laptop and retry once from File Explorer (not from an old shortcut).

Contact the service desk if access is still denied after these steps. Share: your username, drive name (S: / Finance), exact error message, a screenshot of the message, time you tested, and the file or folder you were trying to open.

## OneDrive missing files or sync error
Try these steps first:
1. Open OneDrive from the taskbar cloud icon and click Resume syncing if it is paused.
2. Check OneDrive on the web to confirm whether the file is there, then search the file name in your OneDrive folder.

Contact the service desk if files are still missing or sync still shows an error after these steps. Share: your username, file names affected, where the files were last seen, screenshot of the OneDrive status icon/error, and the deadline tied to those files.
