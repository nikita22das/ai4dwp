## Overview

When a new or reissued device is switched on for the first time, it goes through an automatic setup process. Occasionally this process does not complete and the device becomes stuck on a setup or welcome screen. Your data is safe — nothing has been lost. This article helps you recognise the issue and describes the simple steps you can try before contacting the Service Desk.

---

## Symptoms

You may be experiencing this issue if:

- Your device is stuck on a setup, welcome, or loading screen and does not progress.
- You see a message saying setup could not be completed or that there was a problem setting up your device.
- You are unable to reach the screen where you type your password to log in.
- The device was previously used by someone else before it was given to you.

---

## Steps You Can Safely Try

Try these steps in order. Stop and contact the Service Desk if the problem continues after Step 2.

**Step 1 — Restart the device**
Hold the power button until the device turns off, wait 30 seconds, then turn it back on. Allow 5–10 minutes for the setup screen to progress before trying anything else.

**Step 2 — Check your internet connection**
Make sure the device is connected to your office internet connection or your home Wi-Fi. Setup requires internet access to complete. If you are working from home, ensure your home internet is active.

If the device is still stuck after both steps, do not attempt anything further. Contact the Service Desk — this is a quick fix for our team.

---

## When to Contact the Service Desk

Contact the Service Desk straight away if:

- The device remains stuck after restarting and checking your connection.
- You see any message saying setup failed or your device could not be set up correctly.
- The device was previously used by a colleague and has never fully set up for you.

---

## Information to Include in Your Ticket

Having this information ready will help us resolve your issue faster:

- Your full name and department.
- The number on the sticker on the bottom of your laptop or computer (this is the device identification number used by the IT team).
- Whether the device is new to you or was previously used by someone else.
- A brief description of what the screen shows when it stops.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is my data safe?**
Yes. The setup issue does not affect any of your personal work files or emails. Everything is stored securely and will be accessible once your device is set up correctly.

**How long will it take to fix?**
In most cases our team can resolve this quickly once you report the issue. We will keep you updated and let you know when your device is ready.

**Can I use another device in the meantime?**
Yes — please speak to your line manager or contact the Service Desk and we will do our best to arrange a temporary device while yours is being set up.
