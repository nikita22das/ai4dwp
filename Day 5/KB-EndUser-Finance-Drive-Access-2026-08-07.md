# I Cannot Access My Finance Drive — What Should I Do?

---

## 1. Overview

Your Finance drive may not be showing when you sign in. Our IT team is aware and working on it.

**Your files are safe.** Nothing has been deleted and your permissions have not changed. This is a display issue caused by a recent background change. Once resolved, your Finance drive will return exactly as it was.

---

## 2. Symptoms

- The Finance drive (**S:** or **Finance**) is missing from your computer.
- You cannot open Finance folders you normally use.
- You see an error when trying to access Finance files.
- The drive was visible yesterday but has disappeared today.

---

## 3. What You Can Do

Try these steps in order. Stop and contact the Service Desk if anything is unclear.

1. **Save any open work** and close all programs.
   You are about to sign out — saving first protects your work.

2. **Sign out of Windows.**
   Click the Start button, click your name or profile picture, then click **Sign out**.

3. **Wait 30 seconds**, then sign back in with your usual username and password.
   This gives your computer time to apply the latest settings.

4. **Open the folder icon on your taskbar** and go to **This PC** (or **My Computer**).
   Check whether the Finance drive is now visible.

5. **If the drive is still missing**, restart your computer fully.
   Click Start, then click the power icon, then click **Restart**.

6. **Sign back in** after the restart and check **This PC** again for the Finance drive.

7. **If the drive is still missing after the restart**, stop here and contact the Service Desk.
   Do not attempt anything further — the Service Desk will take it from here.

---

## 4. When to Contact the Service Desk

Contact the Service Desk if:

- The Finance drive is still missing after signing out, signing back in, and restarting.
- You receive an error message when trying to open the drive.
- Other colleagues in Finance are also reporting the same issue.

**Please have the following ready when you call or email:**

- Your full name and department (Finance)
- The username you use to sign in
- Your computer name (usually on a label on the machine)
- The time you first noticed the issue
- Whether you have already signed out and restarted
- A description of any error message you saw

---

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions

**Is my data lost?** No. Your files are stored centrally and nothing has been deleted.

**Has my access been removed?** No. Your permissions are unchanged.

**Do I need a new password?** No. Your password is not affected.

**Do I need a new laptop?** No. This is not a hardware issue.
