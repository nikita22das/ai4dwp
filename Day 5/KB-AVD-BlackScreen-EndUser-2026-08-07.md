# End User Help - Screen Stays Black After Sign-In

## Version Header

- v 1.0
- Date: 07/08/2026
- status: Draft

## What you may see

You sign in, but the screen stays black, or you get signed out and asked to sign in again.

## What to do

1. Wait for 2 minutes.
2. If the screen is still black, use the sign-out option.
3. Sign in again once.
4. If the same issue happens again, stop trying and call the service desk.
5. If possible, use your phone or another device to report the issue.

## Your data is safe

Your files and work are not lost by this issue. Do not keep trying sign-in many times. That can make the problem harder to fix.

## Tell the service desk

Please share:
- your name
- the time the issue started
- what you saw on the screen
- whether you were able to sign in at all
- whether the problem happened more than once
- any message you saw
- the app or work you were trying to open

## Contact the service desk if...

Contact the service desk if the screen stays black after sign-in, you are sent back to the sign-in screen more than once, or you cannot get to your work after one retry.
