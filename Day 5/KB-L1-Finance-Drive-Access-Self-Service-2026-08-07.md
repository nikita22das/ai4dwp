# Finance Folder Not Showing? Quick Self-Help

If your Finance folder is missing, do not worry. Your files and data are safe.

## What You Can Do Now
1. Save your work and close any open programs.
2. Sign out of Windows completely.
3. Wait 30 seconds.
4. Sign back in.
5. Click the folder icon on your taskbar, then go to the My Computer area.
6. Check whether the Finance folder is visible.
7. If it is visible, open it and continue working.
8. If it is still missing, restart your computer once.
9. Sign back in after the restart.
10. Go to the My Computer area again and check for the Finance folder.
11. If it is still missing, wait 5 minutes and check once more.

## Important Reassurance
- Your documents are not deleted.
- Your department files remain protected.
- This issue only affects whether the Finance folder appears on your screen. Your data is untouched.

## If You Need Help
If the Finance folder is still missing after the steps above, call or email the Service Desk.

## Information to Have Ready When You Contact the Service Desk
- Your full name
- The username you type when signing in
- Your department (Finance)
- Your computer name (if known — usually shown on a label on the machine)
- The time the issue started
- Whether you signed out and signed back in
- Whether you restarted your computer
- Whether the folder appeared at any point
- A photo of your screen showing the issue (taken with your phone if needed)
