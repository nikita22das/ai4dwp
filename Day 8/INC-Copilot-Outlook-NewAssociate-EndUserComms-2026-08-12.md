# End User Communication – Copilot Cannot Find Case Emails

**Version:** 1.0
**Date:** 12/08/2026
**Status:** Draft

---

Hi,

Thank you for getting in touch. We completely understand how frustrating this must feel, especially in your first week.

**Why this is happening**
Copilot in Outlook can only work with emails and information that are already available to your account. As a new starter, your account is still being set up — it can take a couple of days before Copilot can see everything in your mailbox. Additionally, some case emails may sit in shared team mailboxes that you may not have been added to yet.

**What to check first**
Try searching for one of the emails directly in Outlook (the standard search bar, not Copilot). If it appears there, please let us know. If it does not appear, your account is likely still catching up — this usually resolves within 24–72 hours.

**If you believe you should have access**
Contact the Service Desk and provide:
- Your name and start date
- The name or email address of the shared mailbox or case folder you need access to
- The name of your line manager

We will get this sorted for you as quickly as possible.
