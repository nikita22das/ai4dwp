Title: Floor 6 Business Update - Monday Incident
Version: 1.0
Date: 14/08/2026
Author: Nikita
Status: Draft

What Happened

This morning, a number of people on Floor 6 reported difficulty signing in or experienced unusually slow access to their devices and work tools. Some users also reported that items they normally expect to see on their desktop were missing.

A separate concern was raised by a member of the Legal team who reported seeing legal matter information through the assistant tool that they did not expect to be able to view. That concern is being treated as a serious matter and is being reviewed separately through the appropriate process.

At this stage, we are describing what has been reported and what has been observed. We are not assigning blame, and we are not yet making final conclusions about cause. We want to be clear that no conclusions are being withheld; some answers are simply not final yet because the remaining checks must be completed properly.

What We Are Doing

We are managing this as a live business incident and are reviewing the reported issues as separate but related workstreams so that each can be handled properly. Immediate action has already been taken to reduce disruption for affected users while the review continues.

Our teams are working directly with impacted users, checking whether the steps already taken are improving sign-in speed and general device performance, and identifying whether any users still need priority support. We are also giving separate attention to the concern involving legal matter information so that it is reviewed through the right business and security process. Work is ongoing, and updates will continue as the review progresses.

What Remains Open

Some parts of this incident are still under review. We are continuing to validate why users experienced sign-in delays, whether the missing desktop items are part of the same issue or a separate one, and whether the concern involving legal matter information reflects an access issue or another explanation.

Additional validation is required because early reports describe user impact, but not every report points to the same underlying cause. It is important that we confirm the facts carefully before giving final answers. The concern relating to legal matter information is being treated seriously and is being reviewed through the appropriate process alongside the wider incident response.

What Users Should Do

Users should continue normal work where possible. If problems continue, they should report them to the Service Desk with a short description of what they are seeing and when it happened.

Users who still cannot sign in, are experiencing unusually slow access, or continue to see missing desktop items should contact the Service Desk so they can be prioritised for follow-up. Anyone who believes they have seen information they should not have access to should report that immediately. This will help us support users quickly while the wider review continues.
